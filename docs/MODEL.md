# Model reference

Developed and maintained by [IntrAgile Kft.](https://intragile.eu)

Where each EN 16931-1 business term (BT) and business group (BG) lives in the
`IntrAgile\EInvoicing\Model` namespace. Generated by `bin/generate-model-doc.php` from the
PHPDoc of the model classes; do not edit by hand. A row without a property is the class
that represents the whole group. Where a property documents several ids (for example
seller, buyer and payee names), it appears once per id.
